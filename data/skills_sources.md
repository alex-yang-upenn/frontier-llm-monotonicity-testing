# Skills data: Source and Rationale

The skills section was primarily built by surveying common skills on current LinkedIn content for Software Engineers and ML Engineers. 

Additionally, we referenced Stack Overflow 2025, GitHub Octoverse 2025, JetBrains 2025, and resume-keyword studies.

To maintain our resume structure and try to achieve at least superficially equal levels of relevancy, we picked skills that are commonly listed in a tech-stack format, and that are somewhat related to the job posting. We avoided soft skills, overly niche or enterprise-specific items, and overly general phrases.
